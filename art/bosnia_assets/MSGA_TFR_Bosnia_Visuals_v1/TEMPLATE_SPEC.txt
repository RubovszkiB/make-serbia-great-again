MSGA / TFR — DIVISION TEMPLATE SPEC

1) Territorial Defence Militia
- Editable by the player.
- Template composition: 3 x Militia battalions ONLY.
- No support companies.
- No artillery.
- No motorized.
- No APC / IFV.
- No tank battalion.
- The focus spawns 1 Territorial Defence Militia division in Belgrade.
- Regional Territorial Defence decisions each cost $0.5B, are one-time per eligible Serbian state, and spawn 1 division using this same template.
- Intended decision duration: 14 days.

2) Republika Srpska Militia / Srpska Guard
- Template composition: 3 x Militia battalions ONLY.
- No support companies.
- No artillery.
- No motorized.
- No APC / IFV.
- No tank battalion.
- When Republika Srpska is released, spawn 3 divisions using this template.
- Keep it as a normal editable division template unless an existing TFR mechanic explicitly requires otherwise.

IMPORTANT:
Do not preload either template with tanks, APCs, IFVs, artillery, engineers, recon, or other support.
The whole starting template is only three plain militia battalions.
