# Balkan Encirclement 0.11 — installed pre-war chapter

Implemented in the primary installed mod first, validated there, then synchronized to source. This increment ends at the opening of the Pact war. Engine gameplay remains reserved for the user's later test; no running game window or save was modified.

1. **Files changed.** 46 files inside `C:/Users/Balazs/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/make_serbia_great_again`, plus the sibling `make_serbia_great_again.mod` launcher descriptor. The complete absolute list is `deployed_absolute_paths` in [encirclement_sources.json](encirclement_sources.json). This includes two focus trees, one trigger file, four new effect/on-action files plus the capstone effect update, two event files, ideas, English localisation, six OOBs, GFX, 25 DDS textures and both internal descriptors. [encirclement_sync.json](encirclement_sync.json) verifies all 261 source/runtime files. Workshop ID `3813570241`, dependencies and the actual launcher path were preserved. No TFR installation, other mod or save was edited.

2. **New focus trees.** `MSGA_SER_southern_question` contains only `MSGA_pressure_podgorica` and `MSGA_pressure_skopje`, each 14 days. `MSGA_SER_pact_war_planning` contains the ten planning focuses. Earlier trees and the 12-focus, 168-day post-Bosnia chapter retain their layouts and rewards.

3. **Switch and reveal.** The existing regional-power capstone sets `MSGA_regional_power_achieved`, then calls `MSGA_open_southern_question`. Startup and weekly recovery also recognize saves already at this milestone. Entry clears `MSGA_post_bosnia_active` and uses `load_focus_tree` with `keep_completed = yes`. The planning tree loads only after `MSGA_zagreb_tirana_pact_formed`, `MSGA_pact_mobilised` and `MSGA_belgrade_emergency_done`. It is absent from the active tree before then. Recovery uses weekly hooks, without daily polling.

4. **Rejection flags.** Serbia receives `MSGA_montenegro_rejected` from `MSGA_encirclement.1` and `MSGA_macedonia_rejected` from `.2`. Each event immediately applies −10 PP and +2 percentage points war support. Either focus can come first. Both flags schedule `.3` exactly three days later, once; it applies −5 percentage points stability, +5 war support and `MSGA_calculation_failed`.

5. **Actual TFR NATO implementation.** Membership is an engine faction, with the changing leader recorded by `NATO_current_leader`. The USA GUI additionally uses `USA.USA_nato_members`. Native invitation history uses `has_joined_NATO_by_event`; the AI strategy `seek_NATO_membership` explicitly checks `left_NATO`. Native `NATO_unity_1` through `_5` spirits apply NATO cooperation. `NATO_join_faction_effect` sends `nato.4` to the recorded leader. These sources were read from installed Workshop TFR, not assumed from country names.

6. **Safe removal.** For each of CRO, ALB, SLV, MAC and MNT, the actual current faction leader removes only that country if it is currently in that faction. Existing `USA_nato_members` entries are removed individually; `left_NATO` is set, invitation history and stale NATO-leader flags cleared, and all five unity spirits removed. A Pact independence idea prevents AI faction requests. The local override of `events/TFR_events_ZZZ_NATO.txt` adds exactly three guards: `.1` rejects invitations to a Pact member; `.2` and `.4` reject applications/acceptances from one. Removing these guards reconstructs the native AST exactly. The upstream hash is recorded for rebase. External guarantees of the five prospective opponents are removed at realignment and checked again at war opening. Guarantees of Serbia, Bosnia and all unrelated countries are preserved. The original NATO leader and its remaining members are preserved.

7. **Faction.** Croatia creates `MSGA_zagreb_tirana_pact`, localized as Zagreb–Tirana Pact; Albania joins as co-founder, followed explicitly by Slovenia, North Macedonia and Montenegro. No AI diplomacy is required. Serbia gets one Brussels realignment event and one Pact event, rather than five repetitive withdrawal stories. Narration treats the Pact as a defensive sovereignty alliance. Candidates must exist, be independent and peaceful, and not lead an unrelated faction; the script does not dismantle a faction or force a subject to break free.

8. **Tags.** Croatia `CRO`, Albania `ALB`, Slovenia `SLV`, North Macedonia `MAC`, Montenegro `MNT`, Serbia `SER`. Slovakia is `SLO` and is not involved. Existing subjects remain Bosnia `BOS`, Herzegovina `HRZ`, and optional Republika Srpska `SRP`.

9. **Militia template.** Native TFR `militia` at `(x=0,y=0)`, `(0,1)`, `(0,2)`; no support companies or other regiments. Native width 9, 3,000 men, 600 infantry equipment and 15 support equipment per division. The support equipment is a native battalion requirement, not an added support company.

10. **Motorized template.** Native TFR `motorized` at `(0,0)` through `(0,3)`; no support companies or other regiments. Width 12, 4,800 men, 1,000 infantry equipment, 160 motorized equipment and 80 support equipment per division. `motorised_infantry` enables this actual battalion. All three motorized recipients already possess that native technology. Neither `mot_militia`, APCs nor tank supports replace the supplied screenshot design.

11. **Spawn counts.** Croatia 5 militia + 2 motorized; Albania 3 + 2; Slovenia 3 + 1; North Macedonia 3 militia; Montenegro 3 militia. Total 17 militia + 5 motorized = 22 Pact divisions. Serbia receives two militia separately. Once flags and OOB guards prevent repeated grants.

12. **Locations.** The installed TFR country histories, state ownership/cores, province lists and capital victory points were checked. Croatia: state 109, province 11581; Albania 44/9914; Slovenia 102/9627; Macedonia 106/3882; Montenegro 105/9809; Serbia 107/11586. Before deployment each country must own, fully control and have a core on its capital state, and control the exact city province. Unsafe locations delay the grant and weekly recovery retries it. No units spawn into occupied enemy provinces.

13. **Equipment/manpower.** OOBs use `start_equipment_factor = 1`, `start_manpower_factor = 1` and experience 0.1. Full Pact formation requirements are 75,000 men, 15,200 infantry equipment, 800 trucks and 655 support equipment. Serbian emergency formations require 6,000 men, 1,200 infantry equipment and 30 support equipment. There is no second stockpile grant duplicating this OOB equipment. Native `has_manpower` checks add at most the formation requirement per recipient, only if its existing pool is below that requirement. This is a bounded sufficiency grant, not an exact shortfall calculation; countries with enough manpower receive zero. Actual engine strength and stockpile accounting still require the user's test.

14. **Belgrade response.** Approximately one day after faction creation, `.6` mobilises the Pact and queues `.7`. The Belgrade response adds one shared slot and one military factory in state 107, plus the two emergency militia at 11586. Its once flag is set before tree reveal. Existing Belgrade factories and previously granted militia remain intact.
