# Balkan early mini-trees — installed 0.17.0

LIVE was implemented and validated before source synchronization. Bosnia, North Macedonia and Montenegro each receive six peacetime focuses and eight emergency focuses: 42 total. Bosnia has five one-time contracts; Macedonia and Montenegro have four each. There are 23 moderate ideas and 50 supplied-image DDS. Exact IDs, days, rewards, native references, state/province geometry, individual archive members, crops and installed paths/hashes are recorded in `balkan_mini_sources.json`; complete runtime/source verification is in `balkan_mini_sync.json`.

The new `on_declare_war` hook requires Serbian ROOT, Kosovo FROM and the existing Serbian `MSGA_kosovo_war_started` flag, set by the original effect alongside the actual declaration. It immediately sets each country's permanent `MSGA_mini_rearmament_unlocked`. Startup/weekly migration handles existing saves and later-existing countries without daily events. Availability also accepts the persistent original flag directly. The country unlock survives active-war cleanup and Serbia flag cleanup. No uprising, Bosnia war or intervention is started by these mini-trees. Serbia's original Kosovo and Bosnia files are unchanged.

The native assigned `BOS_2020` OOB retains its four original divisions and templates. It appends the existing three-militia `Bosnian Territorial Brigade` template and exactly two named units, relocated from the original Serbian auxiliary recruitment site to Sarajevo (11899). Units are created by fresh-campaign history only, never by startup, weekly migration or reload. The later, separately paid Serbian protectorate auxiliary recruitment remains unchanged. Macedonia/Montenegro starting forces remain unchanged.

Bosnia's armour contract delivers 100 of the original **SER T-55A**, with `variant_name`, using `modern_tank_chassis_1` under NSB and `modern_tank_equipment_1` otherwise. The Serbian initialization flag and surviving supplier are checked; no new variant is created. Completion creates only **Bosnian Armoured Brigade**, capped at one division. Native modern armour consumes 50 tanks per battalion, so one tank battalion plus three motorized, engineers and support artillery uses 50 tanks and leaves 50 for reserves. Two native tank battalions would consume the entire shipment; a 70–90 requirement cannot be expressed by whole native tank battalions without altering TFR units. The template additionally needs 120 trucks, 775 rifles, 110 support equipment, 12 artillery and 4,550 manpower, derived from native unit definitions. Recruitment preference requires all these resources; no free armoured division is spawned. Its actual engine training remains untested.

Montenegro's focus creates the existing **MSGA MNT Territorial Militia** three-militia template only if absent, supporting ordinary AI recruitment when 600 rifles, 15 support equipment and 3,000 manpower exist. It spawns no units. The later approved Pact emergency OOB is unchanged. Macedonia and Montenegro reserve ideas use native country `conscription_factor` (+3% / +2%) and `mobilization_speed` (+10%), rather than flat manpower or spawned divisions. Native TFR manpower laws use this country modifier; reinforcements use `land_reinforce_rate`, training uses `training_time_army_factor`.

Bosnia gains two MIL through the emergency branch in core state 104; Macedonia gains one in 106 and Montenegro one in 105. All require ownership/control and native capacity. Shared slots are granted first; factory delivery uses native before/after counters, rejection logs and slot rollback. Infrastructure stops at native maximum five; Macedonia's second infrastructure reward therefore cannot raise its already-maximized state beyond five. Forts use actual map geometry and province capacity: Sarajevo 11899 (+2) and northern approach 6799 (+1); northern Macedonia 3882/907 (+1 each); northern Montenegrin mountain provinces 11858/6913 (+1 each).

Contracts reserve native `income_var` Treasury once via `income_var_temp`/`add_income`. Persistent pending/completed/shared busy flags prevent duplicate payment, repeated delivery and parallel shipments. Cancellation refunds once and permits retry; completed contracts disappear permanently. AI retains a $0.25B reserve, prioritizes cheap stock and gives the T-55 contract greater priority when sufficiently funded and armour-poor. Descriptions/tooltips show cost, time, exact equipment and one-time status; executable guards/effects are hidden.

Every supplied individual image is used; preview sheets are excluded. Missing Bosnia budget art reuses the supplied Bosnian war-industry picture; missing Macedonia/Montenegro final-focus pictures use their supplied final-spirit scenes. Stray caption strips above five Bosnian crops are trimmed without repainting. Focuses use inspected installed MSGA 95×85 DXT5 canvases, spirits 64×64, with focus/shine GFX. Inspected TFR native images use varying sizes, including 60×68 idea DDS; current MSGA conventions remain intact. The supplied Kosovo-alarm and T-55 images provide the Bosnia category and tank-contract icons. No new art or yellow border is generated.

The seven-country native AI restoration, Albania/Croatia trees, all Serbian chapters, endgames and economic/energy systems retain exact prior hashes. Only the two existing descriptors change among the prior 602 files; 65 paths are new. Workshop identity and the actual runtime launcher path remain intact, with all 667 source/runtime files matching after sync.

Targeted LIVE checks passed: the new validator (three full focus routes, fourteen delivery/DLC models, 46 negative cases), phase1, Kosovo, Bosnia and the previous Balkan rearmament validator. The old Kosovo validator was updated only to preserve its original eight-tree/80-focus coverage while accepting separately added trees, unwrap approved decision presentation and locate the volunteer category by content rather than an obsolete numeric index. The old Balkan validator now consults exact approved descriptor hashes. No game logic was changed to satisfy these checks.

**Engine validation was not run.** The user's game window and saves remain untouched.
